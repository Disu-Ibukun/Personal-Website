01A little about me

I care about what data tells us. I'm just as interested in what makes it usable.

Data can help us understand what happened, see what is happening, and notice patterns that might inform what happens next. That range of possibilities is what first made me curious about it.

But data doesn't become valuable simply by existing. It has to be collected, organised, processed, stored, and made accessible. I'm drawn to Data Engineering because that work sits between information and the decisions people need to make.

Outside technical work, I enjoy public speaking, debate, and bringing people together to make ideas happen. These aren't separate from how I approach engineering; they've taught me to ask better questions, communicate clearly, and think about the people relying on a system.

Curiosity is the starting point.
